colour-graded. These are representative images for fictional sample markets,
not photographs of verified FreshFind locations.

The 22 `produce-*.jpg` images are custom AI-generated, stylized 3D food still
lifes made for this demo, not product photographs. They were composed in four
contact sheets, then cropped and optimized locally. No external image service
is required at runtime.

`logo-leaf.png` is an existing project asset, unchanged in this update.
